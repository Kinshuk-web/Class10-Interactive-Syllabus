Introduction: What is Trigonometry?

Trigonometry is the branch of mathematics that studies the relationships between the sides and angles of triangles, especially right-angled triangles...

Why is trigonometry important? Engineering, architecture, navigation, and astronomy all rely on these principles.

Understanding the Right-Angled Triangle

Consider a triangle ABC that is right-angled at B:

  • Hypotenuse: The longest side, opposite the right angle.
  • Opposite side: The side opposite to the angle in question.
  • Adjacent side: The side next to the angle.
